How to read these comparisons

HGAB amounts are proposed allocations, not verified spending. Per-meter figures divide the whole allocation by the length stated in the title; they are screening ratios, not confirmed construction costs. Existing inventory dimensions do not establish funded work quantities.

Inventory condition labels have no assessment dates in the matched rows. DPWH Transparency document counts do not confirm physical completion. Parsed billboard prices do not establish the sign’s required specification, quality or installation.

Sources and exact figures

Length coverage: Roads: 3,329 of 6,482 line items have a usable stated length; bridges: 65 of 1,159. The offline DPWH inventory adds 595 existing road-section length matches and 754 existing bridge spans. These dimensions do not fill missing funded work lengths; 9 road rows have an asset length while the title length is missing.

Per-meter ratios: Roads use 3,329 line items with stated lengths; median ₱34,722.22/m, Q1 ₱21,000.00, Q3 ₱62,500.00. Bridges use 65; median ₱916,666.67/m, Q1 ₱495,867.77, Q3 ₱1,807,142.86. These divide whole proposed allocation by title-stated length. They are not verified construction costs.

Bridge rehabilitation: The review covers 290 rehabilitation/repair rows; 270 have an inventory bridge ID; 226 have a condition label. Condition counts: Good 29, Fair 138, Poor 58, Bad 1, no matched condition 64. The retained DPWH Transparency snapshot shows 3 rows with 5+ documents listed, 254 with fewer than 5, and 33 without a verified title match. That snapshot records document listings, not completed construction; retrieved 2026-10-18T08:46:20Z.

Billboards: In parsed as-submitted POWs, reconciled to Senate HGAB POW rows where available: roads 2,106 projects with a usable “Project Billboard / Signboard · each” rate (median ₱3,871.16; Q1–Q3 ₱3,389.06–₱4,897.33; maximum ₱45,442.69); bridges 189 (median ₱3,554.29; Q1–Q3 ₱3,213.42–₱4,359.46; maximum ₱11,291.94). Item descriptions may cover different specifications; high values need source review.
