Tech Tree · Building levels

Score = sum of the highest level for each named building identity. For example, Level 2 + Level 3 + Level 1 = 6. One Level 2 building plus three Level 1 buildings scores 5. Building counts and levels are record-based review indicators, not verified physical construction.

How to read the levels

Level 1 is the first year a matching normalized title appears in that barangay. Each later distinct fiscal year with the same normalized title adds a level. Identical titles duplicated in one year are kept separate and marked unresolved. Where the title is too generic or duplicated, the page avoids counting that record as a repeat.

ODV project classification, title parsing, and barangay-name resolution can miss projects or leave ambiguous locations unmatched. See source references on each project row. This is a screening inventory, not proof of a physical building, upgrade, payment, or wrongdoing.
