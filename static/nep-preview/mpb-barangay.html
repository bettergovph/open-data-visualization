Scope and interpretation

FY2017–FY2018 use DPWH GAA Explorer rows whose titles contain multi-purpose wording with a building, bldg, or facility term. FY2020–FY2026 use the integrated GAA project records; FY2027 uses HGAB 3rd-reading rows selected by the exact Multi-Purpose Buildings PAP. FY2019 is not included: the available manifest snapshot marks its editions as differing and contains no named DPWH project rows. The table includes every barangay in ODV’s unified location list; barangays without a matched MPB record show zero.

Amounts are listed budget allocations across the ten included fiscal years, not verified expenditures, completed construction costs, or money received by a barangay. A repeat level means the normalized project title appears again in the same official barangay in another included year. Proximity is never used. A repeat title is a review signal and does not prove it is the same physical building or that an upgrade was completed. A line naming several barangays is associated with each named barangay at its full amount; barangay totals therefore overlap.

Tech Tree · Upgrade levels

Score = sum of the highest level for each named structure identity. For example, Level 2 + Level 3 + Level 1 = 6. One Level 2 structure plus three Level 1 structures scores 5. Structure counts and levels are record-based review indicators, not verified physical construction.

Build history · How levels are assigned

Level 1 is the first year a matching normalized title appears in that barangay. Each later distinct fiscal year with the same normalized title adds a level. Identical titles duplicated in one year are kept separate and marked unresolved. Where the title is too generic or duplicated, the page avoids counting that record as a repeat.

ODV project classification, title parsing, and barangay-name resolution can miss projects or leave ambiguous locations unmatched. See source references on each project row. This is a screening inventory, not proof of a physical building, upgrade, payment, or wrongdoing.
