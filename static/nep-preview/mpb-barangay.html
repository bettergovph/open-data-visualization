Build history · How levels are assigned

Level 1 is the first year a matching normalized title appears in that barangay. Each later distinct fiscal year with the same normalized title adds a level. Identical titles duplicated in one year are kept separate and marked unresolved. Where the title is too generic or duplicated, the page avoids counting that record as a repeat.

ODV project classification, title parsing, and barangay-name resolution can miss projects or leave ambiguous locations unmatched. See source references on each project row. This is a screening inventory, not proof of a physical building, upgrade, payment, or wrongdoing.

Scope and interpretation

This page uses BetterGov’s public MPB map line export. FY2015–FY2026 rows use listed GAA allocations; FY2027 rows use House Bill allocations. A row enters a barangay ranking only when the source assigns it an official barangay PSGC code. The source’s place basis may be a title match or GPS placement. Rows assigned only to cities, provinces, districts, regions, or no place are excluded. The list includes official barangays without matched MPB rows, shown with zero allocation.

Amounts are listed budget allocations, not verified expenditures, completed construction costs, or money received by a barangay. A repeat level means a normalized project title appears again in the same official barangay in another available fiscal year. This is a title-based review signal, not proof that the same physical building was constructed or upgraded. A source line assigned to a barangay at its full amount can make the barangay totals overlap.
