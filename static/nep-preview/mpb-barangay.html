Scope and interpretation

FY2015 uses DBM DPWH GAA Capital Outlays PAP titles (grouped by PAP title and office; PAP codes are not unique project IDs); FY2016–FY2018 use title-classified DPWH GAA Explorer PAP rows for multi-purpose buildings; FY2019 uses the DBM technical DPWH GAA UACS-coded schedule, filtered to specific PAP rows with MPB wording; FY2020–FY2026 use the integrated GAA project records; FY2027 uses HGAB 3rd-reading rows selected by the exact Multi-Purpose Buildings PAP. The FY2016–FY2019 extracts are separate historical source layers, and repeated titles are scored only across years loaded here. The FY2019 GAA Explorer manifest snapshot is not used because it lacks named DPWH rows. The table includes every barangay in ODV’s unified location list; barangays without a matched MPB record show zero. Unavailable GAA years appear as disabled selector placeholders and are excluded from building levels, allocations and rankings: FY2000–2008 is partial/aggregate-only; FY2001/2004/2006 had no new enacted GAA; FY2009–FY2015 has no reconciled MPB project rows. This page uses FY2016–FY2019 and FY2020–FY2027 only.

Amounts are listed budget allocations across the twelve included fiscal years, not verified expenditures, completed construction costs, or money received by a barangay. A repeat level means the normalized project title appears again in the same official barangay in another included year. Proximity is never used. A repeat title is a review signal and does not prove it is the same physical building or that an upgrade was completed. A line naming several barangays is associated with each named barangay at its full amount; barangay totals therefore overlap.

Build history · How levels are assigned

Level 1 is the first year a matching normalized title appears in that barangay. Each later distinct fiscal year with the same normalized title adds a level. Identical titles duplicated in one year are kept separate and marked unresolved. Where the title is too generic or duplicated, the page avoids counting that record as a repeat.

ODV project classification, title parsing, and barangay-name resolution can miss projects or leave ambiguous locations unmatched. See source references on each project row. This is a screening inventory, not proof of a physical building, upgrade, payment, or wrongdoing.
