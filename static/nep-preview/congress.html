How districts enter this comparison

Project location and scope determine attribution. The same source, location and NEP/HGAB matching rules apply to every district. Representative names and sourced roles are added after the allocation totals are calculated; they do not change project inclusion, matching or amounts. No person is assigned a special baseline or risk score.

The default view includes all district rows. Rankings follow only the budget metric, order and filters shown above; ties use district name order. The graph shows the first 12 filtered districts for budget/count metrics, or all eligible filtered districts for the share plot. No district is selected automatically for the optional comparison. Committee and majority/minority filters describe the sourced roster and do not establish influence over an allocation.
