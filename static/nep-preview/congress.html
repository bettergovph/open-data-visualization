How to read this report. This report lists every HGAB project line with one or more review flags: NEP insertion/counterpart status, allocation change, office change, repeated allocations, high-allocation peer screens, within-HGAB repeat candidates, and coordinate/location review. Candidates appear under a district only when existing geographic review assigns them there; 2,166 insertion candidates with unresolved/shared locations remain outside district reports; other flagged rows without a supported district assignment appear in the unassigned bucket. The retained Bataan 2nd and Antipolo 1st notes are hand-reviewed. The selected district’s office or location association does not establish representative sponsorship. Unmatched lines and repeated allocations are review leads, not proof of insertion, duplication, or overpricing.

Additional flags to investigate

  • Same-site allocation Two Saint Francis II, Limay lines in the same HGAB schedule: ₱40M on p. 726 and ₱30M on p. 727. Their printed coordinates are about 40 m apart and the project titles are the same. The ₱30M row is the exact NEP counterpart; the ₱40M row has no clear one-to-one counterpart. Check whether the ₱40M is a distinct phase/scope or an additional allocation for the same facility before calling it an insertion or duplicate.
  • Prior phase at same point The ₱40M Bataan Convention Center MPB in Barangay San Jose (p. 727; 14.674898, 120.529098) is at the exact coordinates of a FY2026 ₱100M Phase 8 project. The FY2027 title omits a phase number. Verify completion status, contract scope, and whether this is the next phase; same coordinates alone do not establish duplicate funding.
  • Repeated site name, location unverified The ₱25M Barangay San Jose MPB (p. 727; 14.671365, 120.535013) has same-name historical GAA entries: a ₱50M rehabilitation in FY2020 and ₱10M construction in FY2025. Those older entries have no recovered coordinates, so confirm the specific building and earlier completion before treating these as repeat work.
  • Disclosure snapshot Of 21 Bataan 2nd rows with exact NEP title matches, 19 had zero documents listed in the DPWH transparency snapshot retrieved 18 October 2026. The two with documents were the Bataan 2nd DEO Office Building (5) and its Rainwater Collector System (1). The Saint Francis II ₱30M match (Proposal 35602), Convention Center ₱40M match (35613), and San Jose ₱25M match (35614) each show zero. This is a listing gap in that snapshot, not proof documents do not exist elsewhere.
