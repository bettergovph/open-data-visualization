Disclaimer. Scope: This report collects FY2027 DPWH HGAB 3rd-reading project records and review flags from the source screens described below. Each flag is a prompt to check the cited evidence; it does not establish intent, responsibility, benefit, overpayment, duplicate scope or wrongdoing. An HGAB-only record means the current comparison selected no NEP counterpart; it does not establish that a project is new or identify who proposed it. District and office associations, including the DEO-to-legislative crosswalk, indicate possible coverage only. They do not establish where a project will be implemented, who proposed or requested it, whether it was added during legislative deliberations, or who sponsored, received or controlled funds. Amounts are budget allocations or proposals, not payments. Source documents, data completeness, parsing, matching and boundary choices may affect results. These are preliminary, document-based observations, not allegations or findings of unlawful conduct. Verify cited primary records and seek relevant context before drawing or publishing conclusions.

Sources and limits

  • HGAB 3rd-reading DPWH project schedule, source PDF pages shown in the tables.
  • Local one-to-one HGAB 3rd ↔ NEP comparison: hb_nep_comparison_3rd.csv. “No plausible counterpart” and “scope review” labels are comparison outcomes, not adjudicated findings.
  • Geographic and office association review: congress_geographic_attribution_db_3rd.json and congress_leg_insertions.json. These records assign geographic coverage under the current rules; they do not identify who requested, added, secured, or sponsored an item. Unresolved and shared locations remain unassigned.
  • DPWH FY2027 transparency document listing snapshot: dpwh_transparency_document_coverage.json, retrieved 18 October 2026. Its expected minimum is five listed document types per matched listing; status is a snapshot disclosure check.
