Disclaimer. Scope: This report covers FY2020–FY2027 DPWH GAA/HGAB 3rd-reading records classified by the shared four-category title screen: roads, bridges, flood control and multi-purpose buildings. District totals include only uniquely assigned projects; shared or unresolved locations are excluded and shown in the coverage summary. Where a DEO-to-legislative crosswalk supports an association, it describes office or geographic coverage only; it does not establish that a representative requested, added, secured, sponsored, or controlled the project. Historical project rows are assigned to today’s district boundaries, not historical representatives. Representative names and portraits identify the year-specific roster entry only; they do not identify a project sponsor. Amounts are budget allocations or proposals, not payments. These are preliminary, document-based observations, not allegations or findings that any person, office, contractor or other entity acted unlawfully. A flag, unmatched record, repeated amount or title, price or location outlier, or missing document is a prompt for review; it does not establish intent, responsibility, benefit, overpayment, duplicate scope or wrongdoing. Names and geographic or district associations indicate possible coverage only, not sponsorship, authorship, control, receipt or personal involvement. A DEO-to-legislative district crosswalk, where used, is a preliminary administrative or geographic association. It does not establish where a project will be implemented, who proposed or requested it, whether it was added during legislative deliberations, or who sponsored, received or controlled funds. Source documents, data completeness, parsing, matching, categories, timing and boundaries may affect results.
