Disclaimer. This page compares classified DPWH budget allocations across project categories and years by legislative district. Totals include uniquely assigned project rows; shared or unresolved locations are excluded. Historical allocations use today’s district boundaries. Annual names and portraits use the corresponding Congress roster where the historic seat can be reconciled. Election-year snapshots use the incoming Congress as cohort context, and do not establish budget enactment or sponsorship. Allocations are not payments.

Older GAA coverage. FY2015 project-title candidates come from the DBM DPWH account-level GAA CSV and include capital-outlay PAP records; see the FY2015 parsed lines. FY2016–2018 project-family rows come from the GAA Explorer DPWH baseline; FY2019 project-title candidates come from the DBM technical DPWH GAA schedule and are classified from specific UACS lines. Search all FY2019 DPWH technical GAA lines. FY2000–2008 has partial or aggregate-only coverage; FY2001, FY2004 and FY2006 had no new enacted GAA; FY2009–2013 lacks DEO-level detail and is not reconciled to this district series; FY2014 has no reconciled named-project extract. Unavailable years are disabled and excluded from totals, averages and charts.
