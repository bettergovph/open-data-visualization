Disclaimer. This visualization is based on classified budget records and title-based repeat indicators. It does not verify physical buildings, upgrades, spending, or who proposed or received funds. Check the cited source records before drawing conclusions.

This site is updated as additional source documents are ingested and reviewed. Its records and calculations are preliminary and open to correction. Additional documents, stakeholder input, and independent review can surface missing information, errors, or context and improve accuracy over time; they may support more complete calculations but do not by themselves verify a record. Inviting people to examine the underlying sources and share corrections is part of the purpose of this work. These are preliminary, document-based observations, not allegations or findings that any person, office, contractor or other entity acted unlawfully. A flag, unmatched record, repeated amount or title, price or location outlier, or missing document is a prompt for review; it does not establish intent, responsibility, benefit, overpayment, duplicate scope or wrongdoing. Names and geographic or district associations indicate possible coverage only, not sponsorship, authorship, control, receipt or personal involvement. Source documents, data completeness, parsing, matching, categories, timing and boundaries may affect results. Verify cited primary records and seek relevant context before drawing or publishing conclusions.

How to read this base. Each illustrated MPB building represents one distinct normalized title and official barangay identity. Its level is the highest matched year level through the selected fiscal year. The central tower is only a visual marker for the barangay’s highest recorded MPB level; it does not represent the barangay’s actual town hall or main building. The largest allocation gets the full coin pile; other piles scale to their share of that amount. One coin remains visible even when the listed allocation is zero, with its label showing 0%. These are allocation and title-match indicators, not verified physical buildings, construction progress, or spending.
