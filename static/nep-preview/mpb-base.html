How to read this base. Each illustrated MPB building represents one distinct normalized title and official barangay identity. Its level is the highest matched year level through the selected fiscal year. Townhall level follows the barangay’s highest MPB level; the gold store is filled to this barangay’s allocation as a share of the highest barangay allocation in the same period. These are allocation and title-match indicators, not verified physical buildings, construction progress, or spending.
