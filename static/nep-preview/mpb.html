Coordinate and needs-screening methodology

Haversine great-circle separation, radius 6,371,008.8 meters, between one unambiguous decimal point per title. Latitude/longitude order inferred only from nonoverlapping local screening ranges (4–22 latitude, 116–127 longitude), recorded per point. At least four decimal places required. No geocoding, centroid substitution, nearest-coordinate join or coordinate borrowing from other rows/stages.

  • Printed points are not verified building footprints. Identical or nearby coordinates can be rounded, copied or reference a barangay center. Decimal precision is not positional accuracy.
  • Under-50-meter pairs are interpreted as likely same-site references for scope review. They are not counted as two confirmed buildings. Site identity requires maps, asset IDs, designs or field verification; nearby plots can contain different structures.
  • Municipality containment uses qualified title locations and the archived DPWH ArcGIS municipal boundary layer. Outside points are review candidates: title parsing, printed coordinates or boundaries may be wrong, and no barangay boundary layer was available.
  • A same-barangay identity requires agreement at barangay, municipality and province levels. Matching city wording without a qualified hierarchy is separately marked unverified.
  • One MPB per barangay is a needs-screening hypothesis, not a validated entitlement rule. Different functions, population, capacity, accessibility and hazard exposure may justify multiple facilities.
  • Historical completion/repair allocations can fund the same legitimate building in successive years. A short distance does not establish two separate buildings or duplicate payment.
  • Farther points can support a separate-site interpretation but do not establish need. The 50/100/250/1000-meter bins are descriptive review bands, not corruption thresholds.
  • Pairs are not distinct projects; a cluster of three items yields three pairs. Do not sum pair allocations as losses.

Supporting methodology and limitations

  • Insertion screening uses the same current native NEP/HGAB occurrence matching as the DPWH HGAB page. FAPs are excluded from domestic insertion candidates. A selected one-to-one counterpart is required for an allocation adjustment; approximate title similarity alone does not establish one.
  • Removal candidates are unpaired NEP records in this exact PAP. An absent selected link is not proof of deletion. Changes in wording, office, scope or aggregation can hide counterparts.
  • Repeat comparisons are FY2027 NEP versus available prior-year enacted GAA. The same NEP project can appear in multiple comparison years: comparison pairs and distinct proposals are different denominators. The loaded comparison set covers FY2016–FY2018 and FY2020–FY2026. FY2016–FY2018 is a separate title-classified DPWH project-family baseline; FY2019 named-project detail is unavailable. FY2000–2008 is partial/aggregate-only; FY2001/2004/2006 had no new GAA; FY2009–FY2015 is unavailable or unreconciled. Those years are excluded from repeat counts. HGAB retention follows selected links; a missing link is not a confirmed removal.
  • Directions, tranches and explicit chainage remain part of the inherited repeat screen. Repeated building names do not establish duplicate components or paid work. Barangay/town/province homonyms require qualified geographic evidence.
  • Building types are a simple title-based descriptive split. Other/unspecified includes mixed uses and titles without clear purpose. Equal allocation amounts are not equal unit costs: floor area, design, quantities and site conditions are unavailable.
  • Office totals are administrative allocations, not legislative district totals. Names in project tables come from existing strict PH location DB attribution; blank cells mean unresolved or shared coverage. No name determines screening eligibility.
  • Graphs reflect the current table filters. Ranked project and office graphs show up to 12 rows; table counts and CSV include all filtered rows. Repeat graphs count all filtered comparison pairs by retention status. No hypothesis test or corruption probability is inferred from these descriptive plots.

Request original-page adjudication, approved designs, floor areas, bills of quantities, contracts, project completion and disbursement records before drawing conclusions. MPB findings overlap the full DPWH insertion and repeat findings; their amounts must not be added together.
