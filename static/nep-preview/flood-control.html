2. MPB is the next review target

MPB shows strong repeat and allocation signals, but the available evidence differs from the flood-control contract series. Treat the NEP counterpart results as candidates until each title and source page is checked.

Current reading: MPB is not yet a confirmed second Bulacan-style case. The closest testable lead is a high volume of repeated or similarly titled projects paired with frequent round allocations; broader geographic concentration and year-over-year budget growth still need a like-for-like dataset.

How to read this comparison

  • Known Bulacan pattern: rising project count and contract value from 2022 to 2023, followed by a high 2024 value; Bulacan was the largest single-province flood-control total in each of those years.
  • MPB candidate pattern: large HGAB line-item volume, frequent comparison candidates, repeated historical title matches and rounded amounts. These signals are not independent and do not prove that records describe the same building or funded scope.
  • Not yet measured comparably: the available MPB snapshot does not provide a complete annual MPB time series using the same budget stage and classification for every year. Its office/region distribution is not directly comparable to flood contracts’ province distribution.
  • Next validation: inspect the source pages for the largest MPB candidates, reconcile NEP/HGAB matches manually, verify locations and building types, and build a consistent year-by-year MPB baseline before comparing growth or concentration.
