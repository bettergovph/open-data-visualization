3. An earlier broad surge: Ilocos Norte 1st, FY2022–FY2023

The full-history comparison above is the main screen. This earlier two-year window is shown because it illustrates a broad GAA increase across all four project families; it is not the only period being tested, and it is not the same source or unit as the Bulacan contract chart.

Ilocos Norte · 1st District shows a broad increase. Its categorized project total rose from ₱2.20B across 130 rows in FY2022 GAA to ₱8.51B across 324 rows in FY2023 GAA. 4. MPB: a return after a low year

The all-years comparison shows MPB allocations returning above their all-other-years averages in 2025, 2026, and HGAB 2027 after the low 2024 GAA level. That supports examining a multi-year funding cycle rather than expecting the same level every year. Ilocos Norte 1st is also a district-level HGAB historical high; neither pattern by itself explains the allocation or establishes project need.

Current reading: MPB is not yet a confirmed second Bulacan-style case. The closest testable lead is a high volume of repeated or similarly titled projects paired with frequent round allocations; broader geographic concentration and year-over-year budget growth still need a like-for-like dataset.

7. How to read this comparison

  • Known Bulacan pattern: rising project count and contract value from 2022 to 2023, followed by a high 2024 value; Bulacan was the largest single-province flood-control total in each of those years.
  • Road comparison: NEP 2026 road infrastructure was +61.9% versus the 2020–2025 average in the earlier analysis. In HGAB 2027, assigned national road totals are near the seven-year GAA average, while several individual districts exceed their historical highs. This points to district-level variation, not a nationwide road surge.
  • Full historical comparison: current HGAB 2027 amounts are compared family-by-family with all available 2020–2026 GAA assignments. A new high identifies an amount outside the observed GAA range; it does not explain the change.
  • Ilocos Norte 1st lead: the integrated annual district series shows increases in all four classified families from FY2022 to FY2023. Flood control and MPBs have large absolute increases; roads also rise, while bridges rise from a small baseline.
  • Do not collapse data stages: the Bulacan trend above is based on recorded contracts, while the district scan compares GAA appropriations. They are different views and are not added or treated as equivalent amounts.
  • Not yet matched: local-project clustering inside Ilocos Norte 1st and contractor concentration are not measured in the district budget table. GAA allocations do not identify awarded contractors; contract-level joins and title/location checks are needed.
  • Next validation: inspect source pages for the largest district increases, check locality concentration within each district, reconcile assigned and unresolved rows, and compare project scope and year before describing a recurring pattern.
