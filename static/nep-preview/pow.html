What this investigation can establish

PDF properties such as /Creator and /Producer name software recorded when a PDF was made or last finalized. They may identify a scanner application or device, but they may also identify Excel, a PDF printer, an editor, or a merging service. A single document-level value applies to the whole PDF and cannot tell us whether its pages came from one scanner.

Known processing caveat: signatures were covered using AI before these PDFs were provided. That image editing and any subsequent PDF save or merge may replace the original Creator/Producer values, rewrite image streams, or flatten page content. The findings below describe the supplied, signature-covered copies. Treat every device or software label as a lead about the file’s processing history, not necessarily its original scan source.

Current finding: the corpus contains a mix of office software, PDF tools, and scanner/copier model strings. These are useful leads for selecting documents for manual review. The available structural signals do not prove that different scanners were used, and matching metadata does not prove a single scanner.

2. Embedded image and structure clues

Image dimensions, bit depth, color-space declarations, and compression filters describe image objects stored inside the supplied PDFs. Signature masking can introduce image objects, change compression, or flatten pages; multiple profiles can also arise from different pages, page elements, resampling, or PDF conversion. They are not a device fingerprint. The indexed page orientation and extraction fields can identify documents worth inspecting, but they describe page layout and the extraction process rather than scanner provenance.

3. Do shared metadata tags track a shared PDF workflow?

A Creator or Producer value repeated across unrelated projects and engineering offices is a useful clustering lead. It could reflect a shared contractor, consultant, scanner fleet, office workflow, PDF conversion service, or the later AI signature-masking step. Metadata alone cannot distinguish these explanations or identify the party that prepared a POW.
