Disclaimer. This page compares classified DPWH budget allocations across project categories and years by legislative district. Totals include uniquely assigned project rows; shared or unresolved locations are excluded. Historical rows use today’s district boundaries and should not be read as historical representative attribution. Allocations are not payments.

Older GAA coverage. FY2016–2018 project-family rows come from the GAA Explorer DPWH baseline. They are title-classified current new appropriations and form a separate historical source layer. FY2000–2008 has partial or aggregate-only coverage; FY2001, FY2004 and FY2006 had no new enacted GAA; FY2009–2013 lacks DEO-level detail and is not reconciled to this district series; FY2014–2015 has no reconciled named-project extract; FY2019 editions differ and named-project detail is absent. Unavailable years are disabled and excluded from totals, averages and charts.
