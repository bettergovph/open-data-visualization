Three Ifugao farm-to-market roads after handover

The handovers are documented. Passability through the next wet season is still unknown for all three roads. The DA turned over this group of roads to the local government on October 13, 2022. Their reported lengths add up to 17.56 km. The turnover report identifies the local government as responsible for upkeep.

We used June 2 to October 12, 2023 as the review window, based on PAGASA’s onset and end notices for the southwest monsoon. These dates provide a common window for the pilot. Rain and road incidents can occur outside it.

Bridge access returned in stages after severe weather

At Tagburos, both lanes reopened with clearing work still underway. At Agana, a temporary crossing admitted light vehicles 25 days after the collapse. In Agno, residents relied on boats, makeshift crossings or longer routes before a temporary steel bridge opened.

The dates refer to the reported stages below. In Agno, the starting date is the first disruption shown in the cited record, when the river was rising. The gap runs to the opening of the replacement crossing.

What would establish passability for the three farm roads?

The remaining evidence is the as-built alignment for each farm-to-market road and the 2023 inspection, closure, and repair logs from the local government or DA–PRDP’s maintenance audit. The alignment would establish whether the View Point incidents affected a farm road or a separate approach. Dated closure and reopening entries would make it possible to count days of restricted access by vehicle type.
