Open research question

A completion entry records a construction milestone. A farmer taking produce to market or a family travelling to a clinic also depends on the approaches, drainage and repairs after handover. The current series has no linked record of when each completed road was open, restricted or closed.

What would answer it?

Start with every completed farm-to-market road in one province and identify the road segment, acceptance date and office that took responsibility. Match each segment to dated inspections, closure and reopening notices, and maintenance work orders through its first full local rainy season.

Report days of documented closure or restricted access per segment, the time from a reported defect to restored access, and how many segments have sufficient records to assess. Where records are silent, leave passability unknown. Travel logs from farmers, schools or clinics could establish which trips a closure disrupted.

  1. Identify the completed segment
  2. Verify handover
  3. Check closures and repairs
  4. Measure days of restricted access
