Select a geography and a view below to explore record density and the uses mentioned in MPB titles.

What is counted

Current data and coverage

FY2027 MPB denominator: 5,805 House Bill project rows with PSGC places. Facility counts are dated source records joined to places; they are not counts of MPB-funded buildings.

48,129 DepEd public schools

SY 2025–26 · 47,372 uniquely joined to municipalities and 46,686 to barangays. The panel reports 22,078,460 learners across 48,126 public schools; only joined records appear in place-level totals. Unmatched IDs are excluded from place counts.

44,060 OSM school candidates joined

OpenStreetMap snapshot · 4 Nov 2026 · used as an independent map cross-check, not added to DepEd counts.

2,612 health candidates

OpenStreetMap snapshot · 4 Nov 2026 · 2,682 candidates screened; 70 lack a unique PSGC place.

781 PhilHealth government hospitals / infirmaries matched

September 30, 2026 list · 824 government entries total · 60,744 beds in joined facilities; 43 government rows remain unmatched.

2,722 PhilHealth YAKAP providers matched

September 30, 2026 list · 2,917 government entries total · 195 government rows remain unmatched. YAKAP and hospital listings overlap.

756 evacuation sites / candidates

493 Quezon City government directory sites plus 265 OSM candidates elsewhere · 2 directory sites lack a barangay match.

For school and health records, matching recovered 5,476 and 445 additional municipality joins respectively through unique municipality/province matches or unique matches within a broad province label. Ambiguous records remain unmatched. National school coverage is a joined-record lower bound, and health coverage remains a partial OSM count. The DOH National Health Facility Registry is the stronger next source; its official pages identify the registry and recent reports, but a facility-level download was not available in this research pass.
