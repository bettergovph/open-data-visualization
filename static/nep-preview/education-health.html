Refined evacuation-site counts

We extracted the Quezon City directory into a site ledger, normalized barangay names and removed 3 repeated listings. It yields 493 distinct named sites. Of these, 491 resolve to official barangay identifiers; two retain their published locality for review. The directory replaces the OSM evacuation candidates within Quezon City, so its sites are not added a second time.

Only two listed sites are classified as permanent. The remaining 491 are temporary or semi-permanent, illustrating why designated shelter sites and dedicated evacuation buildings should be compared separately.
