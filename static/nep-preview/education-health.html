MPB · PUBLIC SERVICE ACCESS

MPB vs schools, health facilities and evacuation centers

Compare FY2027 House Bill MPB allocations with public service locations as soon as an authoritative public-only facility inventory is available. This page separates mapped MPB evidence from facility data coverage and title-based purpose clues.

Data coverage: the project currently has no downloadable official public-only school, health-facility or national evacuation-center inventory. Facility counts and facility density are therefore not fabricated. MPB counts below use project locations matched to PSGC.
