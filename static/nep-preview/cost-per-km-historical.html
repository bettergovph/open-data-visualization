Method and limits

Same length parser as current page; positive domestic DPWH OPERATIONS col_J occurrences only from local parsed FY2026 schedule. Peso amounts already in pesos. Official PAP joins use case/punctuation normalization only; unmatched programs excluded from peer comparisons. Lengths below 10m withheld from rate comparisons. Program medians need at least 20 usable records per stage. Length adjustment uses FY2026 length quartiles, at least 20 records per stage per band, and a FY2026-count-weighted mean of band median ratios. Unsupported bands are excluded, never extrapolated.

  • Different projects and stage scope; not paired contracts or measured construction cost inflation.
  • Surface, recorded condition, width, lanes, terrain, structures, geography and funded quantities remain uncontrolled in this cross-year table. Current-page surface/condition screening does not turn this into a matched-context historical comparison. Rates are whole allocation/stated meters.
  • FY2026 column-J leaf scope can omit records at other hierarchy depths; selected coverage is not the complete DPWH budget. Missing metadata can bias comparisons.
  • FMR is not compared here: this historical comparison reads the DPWH schedule only.
  • Medians resist extreme-rate influence; no extreme individual rate dots or bars shown. No formal significance or causal claim.
