Exact ₱5M roads/bridges and flood-control allocations

District offices with one or more project entries budgeted at exactly ₱5,000,000 in the access roads/bridges category or flood mitigation categories. Counts are entries, not distinct projects verified against source documents.

Methods: hypergeometric overlap test and region-stratified randomization test. The hypergeometric test treats the 199 district offices as equally likely to receive each program’s ₱5M entry, fixes the observed counts (96 road/bridge offices and 45 flood offices), and calculates how often random independent placement would produce 35 or more shared offices. The region-stratified test is a second check: it keeps the observed road/bridge offices fixed and repeatedly reassigns the 45 flood-office flags among offices in the same region, preserving each region’s flood count. Its p-value is the share of 100,000 reshuffles with overlap at least as large as observed. Small p-values mean this degree of overlap is rare under these chance models. They do not prove deliberate allocation or a “consolation” motive. The analysis is exploratory because ₱5M and these programs were selected after noticing the pattern; the models also do not adjust for project need, workload, or project mix.
