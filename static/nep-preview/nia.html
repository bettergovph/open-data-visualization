Grouping and source methodology

Source descriptions are retained. All NIA expenditure classes are included: GOCC project subsidies are classified as MOOE in the workbook, so a Capital-Outlays-only filter would miss them. Exact normalized whole-title NIA counterparts support descriptive allocation changes, with source rows preserved; not certified engineering scope. Fuzzy matches require qualified town/province and no explicit conflicts and do not establish changes. Repeated exact titles across grouped identities remain unresolved. Detailed NIA irrigation appendix rows and HFEP facility rows are not compared one-to-one with programme envelopes.

  • Zero difference is possible and is not a parser failure. A nonzero difference can also reflect scope mismatch, incomplete HGAB extraction or a true budget change.
  • Grouped rows conserve workbook amounts but do not certify physical project identity. Positive components, explicit zero and blank catalogue entries remain separate.
  • Current HGAB named NIA rows are not a complete NIA agency ledger. All 32 named row titles/amounts were checked against visible PDF text. The HFEP facility sum reconciles to the printed programme Capital Outlays control, not the programme total including MOOE.
  • No claims of facility-level HFEP insertion or detailed NIA appendix insertion can be made from programme-level NEP entries.
