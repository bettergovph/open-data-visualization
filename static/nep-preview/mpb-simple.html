01FY2027 HGAB · recipient barangays

Most recipient barangays already have earlier MPB records.

Sources & exact figures

3,225 of 4,296 mapped recipient barangays (75.07%) have at least one mapped record in FY2015–FY2026. 1,071 have no earlier match in this dataset. Icons are rounded shares. Earlier allocations do not establish completed buildings or whether further work is justified.

About a third have incomplete document matches.

A missing match does not prove a document is absent; file presence does not verify contents. Document snapshot: 2026-11-09T13:34:24.837779+00:00. The three folders show a rounded proportion.

03FY2027 HGAB · recipients and historical coverage

One barangay does not always mean one project.

Sources & exact figures

The first graphic focuses only on the 4,296 mapped FY2027 recipient barangays. After grouping identical normalized title/barangay pairs, 3,720 (86.59%) have exactly one distinct 2027 title (light blue), and 576 (13.41%) have multiple titles (dark blue). The two groups sum to 4,296; together they have 5,147 distinct title/barangay matches. The number of distinct titles per barangay is counted after the dataset’s title grouping, so repeated identical titles do not increase it. The second graphic uses all 42,010 official barangays as its denominator: 4,296 have a mapped 2027 record (blue), 20,563 have earlier FY2015–FY2026 records but no mapped 2027 record (yellow), and 17,151 have no mapped record in the available FY2015–FY2027 data (grey). These three groups are mutually exclusive; blue includes both returning and first-time recipients. The two graphics use different denominators. These are title groups, not verified physical buildings or findings of duplicate scope. No mapped record does not establish that a barangay has no MPB. Icons are rounded shares; stacked dark-blue icons in the first graphic represent the multiple-title group. This mapped subset differs from the 5,765 exact-program project rows used for document coverage.

04FY2015–FY2026 · available GAA records

Some barangays appear repeatedly. Others do not appear.

Sources & exact figures

Among 42,010 official barangays, 18,222 have no mapped title match in the available FY2015–FY2026 GAA records; 21,151 have one to three distinct title matches; 2,637 have four or more. A normalized title/barangay pair is counted once across those years. No mapped match does not establish that a barangay has no building or received no funding. This graphic excludes FY2027. Icons show rounded shares; stacked icons identify the four-or-more group.

05FY2015–FY2027 records · 2023 poverty rates

Poorer regions tend to get less allocation per barangay.

Sources & exact figures

Each dot is one of 15 comparable regions. The vertical measure is the sum of available barangay-mapped allocations in FY2015–FY2026 GAA plus FY2027 HGAB, divided by all official barangays in that region. The horizontal measure is PSA 2023 poverty incidence among families. Spearman rank correlation ρ = -0.663; two-sided permutation p = 0.0085 (20,000 permutations). The dashed line is a descriptive least-squares trend; its slope is not the Spearman coefficient. NIR, Region VI and Region VII are excluded because the poverty geographies overlap. This regional association does not establish the reason for allocations or measure poverty in each beneficiary barangay. Pooled allocations are nominal and include proposed FY2027 funding. 06PSA · 2023 province and LGU poverty

Do MPB allocations line up with high-poverty provinces?

Sources & exact figures

The bars rank the ten highest provincial poverty-incidence estimates among families in 2023; the smaller line in each row shows mapped MPB allocation per official barangay across FY2015–FY2027 (nominal total; FY2027 is proposed). This descriptive comparison does not establish a funding rule or measure local need. PSA’s 2023 local estimates use small-area estimation and cover 1,611 cities and municipalities, including 14 sub-municipalities in Manila. Eight localities were above 60%; Siayan, Zamboanga del Norte had the highest poverty incidence among population at 67.8%. Locality rates are among population while provincial rates are among families, so the measures should not be compared directly. The local estimates are not joined to specific MPB allocations. Provincial source: PSA 2023 Full Year Official Poverty Statistics. Local estimates, precision measures, and exclusions: PSA city and municipality workbook. The local dataset excludes several highly urbanized cities already covered by direct estimates.

07FY2027 House Bill · DepEd SY 2025–26

How many public schools correspond to each MPB project line?

Sources & exact figures

5,805 FY2027 House Bill MPB project rows compared with 47,372 of 48,129 DepEd public-school IDs uniquely joined to a municipality (98.4%). That is 8.16 joined school records per MPB project row. The datasets overlap by geography and period but do not identify schools served by MPB projects. DepEd unmatched IDs are excluded. Source: NASH Reviewer DepEd SY 2025–26 public-school panel.

08FY2027 House Bill · PhilHealth list, 30 Sep 2026

How do MPB project lines compare with government hospitals?

Sources & exact figures

5,805 FY2027 House Bill MPB project rows compared with 781 of 824 government hospital/infirmary entries uniquely joined to municipalities. This is 7.43 MPB lines per matched government hospital/infirmary. The listings are not matched to the MPB projects and do not measure catchment, need, capacity or construction. The joined hospitals report 60,744 beds. Source: PhilHealth accredited facilities list updated 30 September 2026.
