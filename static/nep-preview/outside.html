How to read this list. This page checks coordinate pairs in the HGAB 3rd-reading DPWH schedule against a broad Philippines coordinate envelope: 4.4°–21.5° N and 116°–127.5° E. The envelope is a screening aid, not an exact national or maritime boundary. Coordinates are reproduced as printed; a mismatch may be a transcription or formatting error and does not establish where work occurred.
