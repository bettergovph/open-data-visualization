Methods and limits

  • Document screen: a link counts when present directly on the HGAB title match or on the selected NEP counterpart. Pairing does not prove the NEP documents have identical scope to the HGAB line.
  • Red flags and cost clustering: exact HGAB ID join to the existing line-item review queue. This carries its repeated-allocation clusters, high unit-cost signals, historical comparisons and related cautions. Candidate flags need source and scope review.
  • Repeated projects: within-stage repeat pairs and prior-GAA title candidates are attached where exact HGAB IDs match. Similar titles or costs alone do not prove duplicate scope.
  • DEO clustering: the review queue's district/representative associations are shown as leads. A DEO may serve multiple legislative districts; the office total is not split or attributed to an individual legislator.
  • LEG crosswalk: the DEO is joined to spatially intersecting legislative districts using the existing boundary crosswalk. Overlap does not assign a project to a district, member, sponsor or controller; boundary dates are unverified and multi-district DEOs remain shared.
  • Senate/GAB removal: only the aggregate 295 / ₱8.836B figure is available in the local inputs. Project-level inclusion remains unresolved until the Senate/GAB rows are matched.
