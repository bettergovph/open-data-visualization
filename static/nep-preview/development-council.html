Scope and extraction limits

Only matched POW and Development Council documents are included. Most names and roles come from best-effort extraction. A small number of image-only POW first pages were visually reviewed and transcribed; those rows are labeled in the source column. Results remain partial: extraction or review may miss or misread names and roles. A person or document missing here should not be treated as evidence that they did not sign or that no document exists. Check the source document before relying on an individual result.

Scope and limits

Sources are restricted to parsed POW and Development Council document matches associated with HGAB project rows. The role labels are extracted candidates and may reflect an “Officer-in-Charge” designation, OCR variation or a historical signature. They are not a verified current roster. A document signature does not show who proposed, sponsored, approved or benefited from a project. Source file paths and direct Senate file links are not published.
