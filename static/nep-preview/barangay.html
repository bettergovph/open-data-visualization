Disclaimer. Scope: This page ranks FY2027 DPWH HGAB 3rd-reading project allocations by barangay names parsed from project titles. A project title can name multiple barangays, so its full allocation may appear under each place; barangay totals overlap and must not be added together as if they were separate shares. Title parsing and locality identification can be incomplete or ambiguous, and an association does not establish exact project boundaries, exclusive benefit, sponsorship, implementation, or actual expenditure. These preliminary records and calculations are open to correction and may change as more source documents are ingested and reviewed. Stakeholder input and independent checks can help identify missing information, errors, or context. Please inspect the underlying project records and share corrections.
