Disclaimer. This is a preliminary, document-based ranking of DPWH project allocations associated with barangay names found in HGAB 3rd-reading titles. A project may name multiple barangays, in which case its full allocation appears under each name; these place totals overlap and should not be added together. Title parsing and locality identification may be incomplete or ambiguous. Project association does not establish exact project boundaries, exclusive benefit, sponsorship, implementation, or actual expenditure. Additional source documents, stakeholder input, and independent review can surface missing information or errors; the records are open to correction.
