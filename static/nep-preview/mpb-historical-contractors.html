How to read this study

  • Project match: punctuation, spacing, and case are normalized; title and year must then match exactly. A single DIME candidate is linked to a GAA line. DIME records without a unique exact GAA match are kept as DIME-only contractor projects and flagged. Awarded PhilGEPS MPB projects are also retained when no exact GAA match exists.
  • Cross-source contractor consolidation: contractor rows group named projects from all three sources by exact normalized company name. The same title/year project is counted once when matched across sources; its source-specific evidence remains attached. Similar company names are not fuzzily merged.
  • Amounts: GAA allocation, DIME contract amount and PhilGEPS award value remain separate. DIME and PhilGEPS amounts are not added to GAA allocations. Repeated PhilGEPS source rows use the maximum reported amount once and are flagged when values disagree.
  • PhilGEPS membership: the merchant snapshot records Red or Platinum membership. The PhilGEPS supplier FAQ distinguishes Red and Platinum; Platinum can expire and downgrade. These cache records were scraped in November 2025 and are not current verification. No “Gold” tier appears in this source.
  • PCAB license: the separate PCAB register provides license number, category, government-infrastructure registration, and valid-through date. PCAB categories (such as A, B, AAA) are not PhilGEPS membership tiers. Company matching is exact after name normalization.
  • AMO leads: company names are matched exactly after normalization against the ODV PhilGEPS/AMO cache. The cache links companies to an Authorized Managing Officer and may carry public-official or relationship fields. Unconfirmed indirect AMO names are masked with first/last-letter hints; names listed as elected officials in the cache remain readable. This does not prove ownership or control.
  • Coverage: the GAA baseline includes classified MPB source lines for FY2015–FY2019 and FY2020–FY2026. FY2015 uses DBM DPWH capital-outlay PAP titles; its PAP codes are not unique project IDs. Search the parsed FY2015 lines. FY2019 uses the DBM technical DPWH GAA schedule; FY2015 and FY2019 appropriations do not identify contractors, so DIME and PhilGEPS matches remain title/year-based and are not implied by GAA source inclusion. Every GAA line is retained in the downloadable inventory, including lines with no DIME or PhilGEPS match. The contractor ranking includes all named DIME records and deduplicated awarded PhilGEPS MPB records identified in the local caches.
