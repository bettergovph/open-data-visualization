Access roads, farm-to-market roads and other bridges

The ₱83 million Siapar–Pilar road and ₱50 million Siapar bridge are separate FY2027 lines with different Senate outcomes. The corridor could give Santiago Island a second mainland connection if the necessary crossing and approaches are built. The available plans and schedule entries leave that connection unresolved.

The FY2026 Liwa-Liwa farm-to-market road has one printed endpoint within about 10 meters of a mapped hospital-route point. It is a candidate tie-in for field verification. Which proposed farm-to-market roads pass a public service?

Of 964 FY2027 House farm-to-market road lines, 489 have at least one mapped public service within 500 metres of the approximate route. Schools appear near 458 lines, health facilities near 55, civic services near 106 and emergency services near 17. A road can serve more than one category.

Of those 489 lines, 339 have their nearest printed endpoint more than 500 metres from a classified national road. Local roads may connect them. Five of the 964 lines have endpoints over 20 km apart and require coordinate review. Measuring the benefit to users requires the actual alignment, a usable entrance and a before-and-after trip.

Data and assumptions

  • Origins use one OpenStreetMap settlement point per barangay. Population counts come from the 2024 Philippine Statistics Authority census. The province lists the existing public hospital in Sampaloc.
  • Road routes use connected OpenStreetMap segments, mapped access restrictions and one-way directions. Speeds are assumed by road class. The ferry crossing uses its mapped 15-minute duration. Ferry waiting time is adjustable. Transfers, schedules, fares, weather and congestion are outside the model.
  • The bridge scenario uses the geotagged mainland and island approach sites from the MGB assessment and straight connections to their respective mapped road networks. The model uses 30 km/h for the crossing and approaches. The House completion line contains a malformed latitude, so the model uses the independent site evidence for the approximate alignment.
  • A bridge would add a second way to cross the channel if ferry service continues. Both options may share access roads. The model does not measure end-to-end route independence or establish that either FY2027 line alone would complete the crossing.
