What the procurement rules allow

Section 12 of the approved implementing rules for RA No. 12009 allows Early Procurement Activities after submission of the NEP, subject to an approved indicative Annual Procurement Plan and the applicable guidelines. The procurement must stop short of award until the appropriate funding is approved and effective and the final procurement plan is approved.

The search compared titles against the notice title, award title and line-item name in 18,515,260 PhilGEPS v2 rows. It matches whole normalized titles after removing accents, case and punctuation. Each title and bid reference pair retains one source row, preferring notice-title evidence. Verify the agency, work scope and location, especially where a short title names only a facility.
