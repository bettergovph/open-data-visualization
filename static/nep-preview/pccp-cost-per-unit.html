PCCP price-list impact

The 2022 DPWH reference lists 11 PCCP road scopes with separate rates for NCR, CAR and Regions I–XIII, plus a listed average. Each scope below has separate box plots for regional reference rates and candidate project prices, with independent axes, plus a region-by-region table. The price-list rate is per kilometer; parsed POW project rates are per square meter. These are displayed together for investigation, not treated as directly equivalent or used to calculate a price gap. Project candidates are matched by PCC pavement wording and thickness only; verify scope, dimensions and source pages.
