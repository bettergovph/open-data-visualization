How to read this evidence
  • Only bill rows with an explicit item heading are included in the cost chart. Quantities retain their original units. Direct costs exclude markup, VAT and engineering/administrative overhead unless the source explicitly says otherwise.
  • The cost columns show the largest three printed category summaries and the largest three specific items within each category, with percentage, total, quantity and unit when recovered. Details lists school or component names, missing numeric fields, unusually small shares compared with the NEP allocation and pages needing manual review.
  • A matching printed direct-cost subtotal establishes that itemized table’s reconciliation only. It does not reconcile the full project allocation, contract or actual spending. Category summaries are not added to itemized rows.
  • Unit prices and amount ÷ quantity are displayed separately. Printed rounding and arithmetic discrepancies remain visible for source review.
  • Signatories require a name next to an explicit role in the same signature column. Assistant district engineers and vice-chairpersons are not substituted. Missing or unreadable evidence remains unassigned; no governor is inferred from geography.
  • Photos open as direct source PDF links. POW/RDC source links include page references. Downloadable records show the values and source references included in this review. Original documents remain at their cited sources.
  • The project evidence table covers the currently reviewed NEP proposal-document set; it is not a complete programme inventory. The coverage and peer sections separately use HGAB 3rd project rows title-matched to the FY2027 DPWH Transparency snapshot; stage scopes may differ. Peer rates are screening comparisons only and do not adjust for project dimensions or site conditions.
  • Access failures indicate retrieval problems, not absent or incomplete official documentation. The coverage note is a dated data snapshot, not a live claim about the entire DPWH programme.
