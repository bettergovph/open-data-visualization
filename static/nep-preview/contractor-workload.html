Sources and coverage

PhilGEPS award dates are joined to the DPWH archive using the contract number and an exact normalized name for a listed license. Conflicting award dates are excluded. Awards to named joint ventures can appear for a matched member and the figures count award–license pairs.

Earlier work spanning the award includes projects with a recorded completion on or after the award date and projects still listed as ongoing at the snapshot. The latter are shown separately in the table. Projects with uncertain earlier status stay visible. Same-day starts are excluded because the order is unknown. Archive coverage begins in FY2016.

The outcome comparison uses completion within 365 days of the recorded start. It compares single-contractor projects with at least 365 days of follow-up, known outcomes, and comparable awards in the same office, category, year and value band. Value bands split at ₱5 million, ₱20 million and ₱100 million. The archive’s completion field and current status cannot establish the original deadline, extensions, or what an awarding office knew. The comparison measures association.
