PhilGEPS download catalog · Public award notices

PhilGEPS award dates are joined to the DPWH archive using the recorded contract number or a single contract ID in the solicitation and title fields for DPWH civil works. The awardee must match an exact normalized name for a listed license. Conflicting award dates are excluded. Awards to named joint ventures can appear for a matched member and the figures count award–license pairs.

Earlier work spanning the award includes projects with a recorded completion on or after the award date and projects still listed as ongoing at the snapshot. The latter are shown separately in the table. Projects with uncertain earlier status stay visible. Same-day starts are excluded because the order is unknown. Archive coverage begins in FY2016.

The outcome comparison uses completion within 365 days of the recorded start. It compares single-contractor projects with at least 365 days of follow-up, known outcomes, and comparable awards in the same office, category, year and value band. Value bands split at ₱5 million, ₱20 million and ₱100 million. Posted end dates come from PhilGEPS. The DPWH completion field can mix planned and actual dates. Confirming a contractual delay requires signed completion and extension records. Both sources can reflect updates made after an award. The comparison measures association.
