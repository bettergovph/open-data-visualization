MPB allocations · FY2015–FY2027

This year-by-year DPWH multi-purpose building series combines the FY2015–FY2019 historical source layers with integrated GAA records for FY2020–FY2026 and HGAB 3rd-reading records for FY2027. Source methods differ by year; “linked” means assigned to an official barangay identity and does not verify a physical site or completed building. See the MPB RTS page for barangay-level detail and methodology, or search FY2015 parsed lines.
