# DSWD evacuation-center source check by region

Checked 12 November 2026. This is a public-source discovery audit, not proof that a field office has no internal or unpublished inventory. “No standing list found” means this research pass did not locate a public, reusable DSWD facility-level list or map for that region. DROMIC incident reports can name/count active evacuation centers during a particular emergency; those are not baseline inventories of all designated centers.

## What is counted

The education-health data includes the FO VII CSV as its own government-source series, with the source limitations above. FO IV-B is a confirmed map lead but currently has no retrieved facility-level extract in this build. OSM and other independent datasets remain cross-check-only. Do not add overlapping DSWD, Quezon City, incident-report or map records together without record-level deduplication and date/status validation.
