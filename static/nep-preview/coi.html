Scope matters. These COIs certify project lists submitted by implementing offices. The HGAB-only figures count House rows with no selected NEP counterpart. Similar office, title, or amount does not by itself prove one-to-one identity between a COI list entry and a House row.

Method and limits

HGAB-only counts and amounts come from the FY2027 House 3rd-reading project rows marked as having no selected NEP counterpart in the local T5 title-linked supporting-document scan. COI project counts and totals are transcribed from certificate cover pages; for the Regional Office VII certificate, the 242 annex proposal codes and amount rows were also checked against the declared total.

The current T5 scan records document presence and title linkage, not complete content verification for every attachment. A copied file record is not counted as a distinct certificate. COI list totals are document statements, not appropriations, disbursements, or validated project costs. Regional comparisons combine scopes and are presented to show scale only.
