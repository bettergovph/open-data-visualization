Scope. Non-MPB records use classified project-family rows with a unique district assignment, then parse barangay names and printed locality from titles. MPB records use the separate official-barangay matcher and include lines without unique district assignments. Some projects name multiple barangays; each place receives the full line amount, so place totals overlap. Title parsing does not verify the construction site, and allocations are not payments.
