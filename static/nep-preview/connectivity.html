Method and limits

  • FY2027 HGAB 3rd FMR schedule: 964 project rows, with printed start/end coordinate pairs for route distance screening.
  • The road comparator is DPWH’s National Road Network layer, including its Primary, Secondary and Tertiary functional classes. These are classes within the national network, not a separate local-road inventory.
  • Distances use straight-line endpoint-to-road measurements in UTM zone 51N. The 100 m, 500 m and 1 km bands are proximity screens, not proof of accessible connection.
  • Farm/cropland proximity remains unmeasured, so the Model / Flag / Double Flag categories are not assigned.
  • For venue review, coordinate-backed DA FMR records are compared with classified visitor venues using a 100 m straight-segment buffer. Route location, operating status and actual access need confirmation.
  • Municipality endpoints are a provisional remote-area proxy. City and unresolved routes are not included in the remote venue matches.
