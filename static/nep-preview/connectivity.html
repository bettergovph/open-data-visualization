Road connectivity

Do FY2027 farm-to-market road endpoints meet an existing DPWH national road? Do remote budgeted road projects sit near non-residential business buildings? The first screen compares mapped FMR endpoints with DPWH’s road network; the second needs better location and building-use data.

1 · Do FY2027 FMR projects connect to an existing road?

Distance is measured from each printed FMR start/end coordinate to DPWH National Road Network line geometry. A close endpoint is a connection lead, not proof that vehicles can enter or that the segment is complete.

Coverage limit: the local ArcGIS layer represents the DPWH national-road network. It does not include every local, barangay, farm, or private road, so “no nearby national road” does not mean “no road connection.”

2 · Are remote budgeted roads near a business building?

Qualifying nearby sites are non-residential buildings used by a business. Exclude government buildings and public markets. A building footprint alone does not identify use or ownership.

Spatial matching is not yet measurable. The table below queues road-budget titles with rural/remote wording, excluding titles that explicitly say “City.” These are prioritization clues, not validated remote classifications. The joined project data has no reconciled coordinates for these GAA/HGAB road works. The local ArcGIS archive also has no nationwide business-building use layer: Microsoft footprints detect structures but do not classify business use or government/public-market status.

Potential business-use sources to evaluate: DTI NegosyoKonek exposes business location and line-of-business fields; BOI Geomaps supports industry searches but says its data cannot be downloaded. Microsoft building footprints supply building shapes, not business-use labels.

Project geotags and business-use evidence are needed before counting nearby sites. Bridge context and resort/business-directory checks remain later extensions.

Method and limits

  • FMR records: 964 FY2027 HGAB 3rd-reading projects, 962 with usable coordinate endpoints in the corrected source ledger.
  • Endpoint distance bands: ≤30 m likely connection signal; 30–100 m manual review; >100 m no nearby line in the DPWH national-road layer.
  • Coordinates are transformed to UTM zone 51N to compare metres with ArcGIS geometry. Distances are straight-line endpoint-to-line distances, not routed access distances.
  • Bridges are not included in this first version.
  • Remote road work is limited to non-city projects once coordinates are recovered; city roads are excluded from that later screen.
