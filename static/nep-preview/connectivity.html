Road connectivity

Do FY2027 farm-to-market road endpoints meet an existing DPWH national road? Are remote budgeted roads near current lodging, resorts or event venues? The first screen compares mapped FMR endpoints with DPWH’s road network. The second uses a 100 m road-alignment buffer, with 250 m as a sensitivity check, once project alignments and current venue locations can be matched.

1 · Do FY2027 FMR projects connect to an existing road?

The screen measures each printed FMR start/end coordinate against DPWH National Road Network geometry and any GAA road-project alignment with usable data for determining distance or connection. The local GAA project tables checked so far have no usable road alignment geometry, so the current 862 red flags are based on the DPWH network only. A close endpoint is a connection lead, not proof that vehicles can enter or that the segment is complete.

Coverage limit: the local ArcGIS layer represents the DPWH national-road network. It does not include every local, barangay, farm, or private road, so “no nearby national road” does not mean “no road connection.”

2 · Remote roads and current visitor venues

Use a 100 m buffer around the budgeted road alignment; show 250 m as a sensitivity check. Count current mapped resorts, hotels, guest houses, short-stay accommodation (including Airbnb-type venues), and event venues. Exclude residential rentals, government facilities, and public markets. A building footprint alone is not business evidence.

Distance and venue counts are pending usable alignments. The budgeted-road records have no project line geometry, so the 100 m/250 m venue test cannot be computed yet. The title candidates below are now checked against the DPWH City-Municipal Boundaries layer. A municipality match is only a provisional remote-area proxy. A project matched to the DPWH National Road Network is non-remote, including national-road corridors crossing city and municipality boundaries. Titles that explicitly identify a national road are tagged as such; without route alignment, other roads stay unresolved. City matches are excluded, and ambiguous/unmatched places stay unresolved.

For current public venue evidence, OpenStreetMap can supply tagged lodging and event-venue locations; its Philippines mapping convention includes hotels and event venues. DTI NegosyoKonek can help verify business identity and line of business, but the public portal is not a downloadable national geospatial inventory. These are leads, not a complete census. Method and limits

  • FMR records: 964 FY2027 HGAB 3rd-reading projects, 962 with usable coordinate endpoints in the corrected source ledger.
  • Question 1 uses endpoint distance bands: ≤30 m likely connection signal; 30–100 m manual review; >100 m no nearby line in the DPWH national-road layer. Question 2 uses a 100 m road-alignment buffer and 250 m sensitivity check when alignments are available.
  • Coordinates are transformed to UTM zone 51N to compare metres with ArcGIS geometry. Distances are straight-line endpoint-to-line distances, not routed access distances.
  • GAA road alignments with usable location data are eligible as additional comparators. The reviewed GAA sources have no usable project geometry (0 of 37,754 integrated GAA road rows), so the 862 current red flags are based on the DPWH network only.
  • Bridges are not included in this first version.
  • The provisional remote-area screen uses municipality/city names from the DPWH boundaries. A route matched to the DPWH National Road Network, including one crossing city and municipality boundaries, is non-remote; without project alignment, other routes remain unresolved. Municipality names are leads, not route-level proof.
