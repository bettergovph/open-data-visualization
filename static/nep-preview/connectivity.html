2 · Remote roads, visitor venues and private properties

Use a 100 m buffer around the budgeted road alignment. Count current mapped resorts, hotels, guest houses, short-stay accommodation (including Airbnb-type venues), and event venues. Exclude residential rentals, government facilities, and public markets. A building footprint alone is not business evidence.

Place inventories are downloaded. Compare these records with the existing road data. City roads and DPWH national roads remain outside the remote-road screen. Municipality location is a provisional proxy; a nearby property is not yet an established road connection.

Method and limits

  • FY2027 HGAB 3rd FMR schedule: 964 project rows, with printed start/end coordinate pairs for route distance screening.
  • The road comparator is DPWH’s National Road Network layer, including its Primary, Secondary and Tertiary functional classes. These are classes within the national network, not a separate local-road inventory.
  • Distances compare printed route legs with DPWH road centerlines in UTM zone 51N, with both endpoint distances retained for review. A route within 50 m is a possible loop/shortcut connection, not a Google driving route or proof of accessible connection.
  • Cropland distances use 2021 ESA WorldCover class 40 pixels around both printed endpoints. Green areas are mapped annual crops, not cadastral parcels. Tree crops and recent land-use changes may be missed. Intermediate combinations remain in Review.
  • For venue review, coordinate-backed DA FMR records are compared with classified visitor venues using a 100 m straight-segment buffer. Route location, operating status and actual access need confirmation.
  • Municipality endpoints are a provisional remote-area proxy. City and unresolved routes are not included in the remote venue matches.
