How to read this evidence
  • Only header-qualified bill rows in this dataset enter the cost chart. Quantities retain their original units. Direct costs exclude markup, VAT and engineering/administrative overhead unless the source explicitly says otherwise.
  • The cost columns show the largest three printed category summaries and the largest three specific items within each category, with percentage, total, quantity and unit when recovered. Details lists school or component names, missing numeric fields, low-share parsing indicators and pages needing manual review.
  • A matching printed direct-cost subtotal establishes that itemized table’s reconciliation only. It does not reconcile the full project allocation, contract or actual spending. Category summaries are not added to itemized rows.
  • Unit prices and amount ÷ quantity are displayed separately. Printed rounding and arithmetic discrepancies remain visible for source review.
  • Signatories require an explicit role bound to a name in the same signature column. DE, ADE, Governor and Mayor candidates are checked in POW/RDC/LDC records; missing or unreadable evidence remains unassigned and no role is inferred from geography.
  • Geotagged photos remain direct source links and remain linked at their source. All ODV-listed document links are exposed; POW/RDC/LDC source links include page references when parsed. The downloadable data contain extracted values, evidence status and source checksums; original PDFs remain linked at their source.
  • The project evidence table reflects NEP proposal documents in this dataset. The coverage and peer sections separately use HGAB 3rd project rows title-matched to the FY2027 DPWH Transparency snapshot; stage scopes may differ. Peer rates are screening comparisons only and do not adjust for project dimensions or site conditions.
  • Access failures indicate retrieval problems, not absent or incomplete official documentation. The coverage note reflects a dated data snapshot, not a live claim about the entire DPWH programme.
