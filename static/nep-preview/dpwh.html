Disclaimer. POW rows are machine-extracted candidates from locally cached proposal documents, not a reconciled project cost, contract price or proof of spending. A recovered largest item may be incomplete or misread; unusually small top-item shares can indicate extraction failure. Verify descriptions, quantities and amounts on the cited source page.

These are preliminary, document-based observations, not allegations or findings that any person, office, contractor or other entity acted unlawfully. A flag, unmatched record, repeated amount or title, price or location outlier, or missing document is a prompt for review; it does not establish intent, responsibility, benefit, overpayment, duplicate scope or wrongdoing. Names and geographic or district associations indicate possible coverage only, not sponsorship, authorship, control, receipt or personal involvement. A DEO-to-legislative district crosswalk, where used, is a preliminary administrative or geographic association. It does not establish where a project will be implemented, who proposed or requested it, whether it was added during legislative deliberations, or who sponsored, received or controlled funds. Source documents, data completeness, parsing, matching, categories, timing and boundaries may affect results. Verify cited primary records and seek relevant context before drawing or publishing conclusions.

How to read this evidence
  • Only cached, header-qualified bill rows enter the cost chart. Quantities retain their original units. Direct costs exclude markup, VAT and engineering/administrative overhead unless the source explicitly says otherwise.
  • The cost columns show the largest three printed category summaries and the largest three specific items within each category, with percentage, total, quantity and unit when recovered. Details lists school or component names, missing numeric fields, low-share parsing indicators and pages needing manual review.
  • A matching printed direct-cost subtotal establishes that itemized table’s reconciliation only. It does not reconcile the full project allocation, contract or actual spending. Category summaries are not added to itemized rows.
  • Unit prices and amount ÷ quantity are displayed separately. Printed rounding and arithmetic discrepancies remain visible for source review.
  • Signatories require a name next to an explicit role in the same signature column. Assistant district engineers and vice-chairpersons are not substituted. Missing or unreadable evidence remains unassigned; no governor is inferred from geography.
  • Photos open as direct source PDF links. POW/RDC source links include page references. The downloadable data contain parsed values, extraction status and source checksums; source PDFs stay outside Git.
  • The project evidence table reflects locally cached NEP proposal documents. The coverage and peer sections separately use HGAB 3rd project rows title-matched to the FY2027 DPWH Transparency snapshot; stage scopes may differ. Peer rates are screening comparisons only and do not adjust for project dimensions or site conditions.
  • Access failures indicate retrieval problems, not absent or incomplete official documentation. The coverage note is a dated cache snapshot, not a live claim about the entire DPWH programme.
