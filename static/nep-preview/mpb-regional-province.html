3 · What do local poverty estimates show?

The latest PSA city- and municipality-level estimates currently linked here are for 2023. PSA used small-area estimation; the release covers 1,611 cities and municipalities, excludes several highly urbanized cities already covered by direct estimates, and reports uncertainty measures. The highest reported 2023 poverty incidence among population was Siayan, Zamboanga del Norte at 67.8%; eight localities exceeded 60%.

Use the PSA release or download its 2023 city/municipality workbook to inspect local estimates, standard errors, coefficients of variation, and confidence intervals. The downloadable workbook is the authoritative locality-level source; this page does not infer a poverty value for cities or municipalities from regional or provincial averages.
