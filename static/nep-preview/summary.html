NEP → HGAB (House version) → GAB (Senate version) → Bicam (House/Senate reconciliation) → GAA (enacted).

NEP and final GAA provide the published bookends. The investigative focus is how allocations change through House and Senate deliberations and bicameral reconciliation. An HGAB line without a selected NEP counterpart warrants verification against project scope, source pages and later budget versions. A matcher result alone does not establish that a project is new or identify who proposed it.

Parsing and location gotchas

NEP → HGAB (House) → GAB (Senate) → Bicam → GAA are different stages; prior-year GAA is enacted, current proposals are not. Match allocation records at the same hierarchy before comparing money.

  • Printed-total reconciliation catches amount/hierarchy errors but cannot certify title identity or location.
  • Preserve source pages and occurrence counts. One comparison row may group multiple allocations; headers are not projects.
  • Require barangay → town/city → province context. DEO numbers do not identify legislative districts.
  • Keep directional names, tranches, province splits and construction chainage distinct. The confirmed multi-place Butuan/RTR line stays outside strict LEG totals.
  • Strict congressional rankings cover only 41.4% of extracted allocation value. Names indicate geography, not authorship or sponsorship.

    How the ranking is calculated

    1. No selected NEP counterpart: the current matcher identifies no plausible NEP counterpart; ambiguous links and FAPs are excluded.
    2. No listed documents: no matched DPWH Transparency entry with listed documents exists in the current snapshot. This is independent of NEP counterpart status; documents may exist elsewhere.
    3. Incomplete documentation: each absent POW, geotagged-photo or RDC type earns one point only when that project attachment list was locally inspected. No type is inferred missing for uninspected projects.
    4. Repeated allocation amount: the same exact positive amount occurs in the same PAP at least 10 times across at least 3 named implementing offices. Roundness alone adds no point.
    5. Historical title recurrence: an FY2020–FY2026 DPWH GAA title agrees after case/punctuation normalization and removal of complete trailing station/coordinate metadata, with qualified municipality and province agreement. FY2020–FY2025 use ODV annual GAA data; FY2026 uses the DPWH Details Enrolled Copy. Work descriptions, directions, packages and phases remain; conflicting locations/tranches and disjoint chainages are excluded.
    6. Historical chainage overlap: an accepted historical counterpart has explicit, positive-length overlap. This is a scope indicator, not proof of duplicate paid work.
    7. Very short range: a recovered printed project length under 10 meters earns a review flag. Verify notation and funded extent; it does not establish an unreasonable allocation.

    Each qualifying type contributes one point, up to twelve possible types. Multiple historical years do not increase points. Historical recurrence and chainage overlap are related signals, so the count is not a statistical probability or independent-evidence score. Missing historical links can reflect conservative matching, incomplete coverage or unresolved names. Standard packages and legitimate amendments can produce these signals. Amounts overlap other findings; do not add them as alleged losses. Names, roles and party affiliations add no points. The complete review queue is also available as full ranking JSON.

    Condition and prior-surfacing flags

    One point for rehabilitation/reconstruction/major repair when the full matched road range was assessed Good/Fair in a known pre-budget year. One point for pavement work when the full matched range has a positive recorded last-surfacing year within five budget years. Widening, approaches, slope/drainage and off-carriageway scope are excluded. Unknowns and partial coverage add no point. The five-year window is a screening choice, not an engineering service-life standard. Bridge Good/Fair context without an assessment date remains a review lead and earns no dated-condition point. Later deterioration or different funded components may justify the work. Condition, recent surfacing and historical recurrence can be related; points are not independent evidence or probabilities.

    Cost-per-meter and short-range flags

    One point when allocation per stated meter exceeds both twice the peer median and Q3 + 1.5 × IQR. Peers share the work category, structure group, matched surface/condition/assessment-year context, exact official PAP and a data-derived length band, with at least 20 records whose stated lengths are at least 10 meters. No fixed 100-meter cutoff is used. Separately, a recovered printed length under 10 meters earns a denominator-warning point; verify the notation and funded extent. Extreme rates remain in this review table with a caution but are excluded from rate charts. Missing lengths and insufficient peers add no point. These flags do not establish overpricing: width, terrain, structures, quantities and funded extent remain unverified. See full rate and grouping methodology.
