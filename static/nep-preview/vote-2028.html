How NO is assigned

NO · Do not vote: two or more distinct elected officials in a source-documented family link held office during at least one same modeled calendar year. Family links come from ODV-verified relationships, shared dynasty-family IDs, or the cited public records listed in the downloaded data. A family consisting of exactly one local and one national officeholder in that year is excluded. Two local officials, two national officials, or three or more officials across levels qualify. A shared surname alone never triggers NO.

This is a Dapat Isa Lang–inspired application of the user’s stated rule; it is not a claim that the movement has adopted this exact data method. The Historical family members column lists linked officials even when their terms did not overlap. That history can inform your decision; it does not automatically mark a row NO.

Term windows use six years for the President, Vice President and Senators, and three years for other included elected offices. ODV election years anchor archived terms; cited online sources anchor supplemental offices and role changes. Repeated copies of the same national office or House seat in one election year are consolidated; different House districts stay separate. Local duplicate IDs are merged only when the full name, office, election year and municipality identify the same seat. If duplicate copies carry conflicting municipality labels, the location is left blank instead of selecting one at random.

The historical election set combines ODV’s elected-office table with the Ateneo Policy Center Political Dynasties Dataset for 2004–2016; matching rows are deduplicated by full name, office, election year and locality where available. The Ateneo dynasty flag is retained as a screening note only, never as proof of a family relationship. ODV congressman display/full-name pairs are retained as aliases only when the pairing is unique. The congressman records were also reviewed: their family_connections field lists contractor entities, so it is excluded from kinship matching. Party-list membership records identify seats, not relatives. Historical 2019 winner-marked data and 2022/2025 local-office coverage remain incomplete. Source-linked kinship records supplement ODV relationships; ambiguous names are not joined. Some source rows or family links can remain incomplete or misclassified. Rodrigo Duterte’s 2025 mayoral win is recorded as an election outcome but is not counted as an office held because he did not take the oath. No NO means this screen did not find a qualifying combination in the available records; it does not establish that a person has no family ties. This is a historical screening aid, not a 2028 candidate list.
