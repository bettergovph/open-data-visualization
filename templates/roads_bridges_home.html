How to read these figures. HGAB amounts are proposed allocations, not verified spending. Per-meter figures divide the full allocation by a length stated in the project title; they are screening ratios, not verified construction costs. Inventory dimensions are context and do not establish funded work quantities. Bridge conditions have no assessment dates in the matched records. DPWH Transparency document counts do not confirm completion. Parsed billboard prices may cover different specifications. Open the source infographic page and methods ↗
