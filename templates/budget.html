📁 Category Statistics

Average cost per kilometer and flagged cost by category and subcategory. Includes Bridges, Road Safety Facilities, Major Roads, and Minor Roads.

Note: Road classification (Major vs Minor) is based on segment count as a heuristic indicator. Roads with ≥10 segments are classified as "Major Roads", while roads with <10 segments are classified as "Minor Roads". Without official documentation, it is difficult to definitively classify roads as national vs secondary.

📊 Flagging Methodology:

Projects are flagged as outliers using statistical analysis:

  • Threshold Formula: Mean + (1 × Standard Deviation)
  • Justification: Using 1× standard deviation provides a balanced threshold that identifies potential outliers. This approach is preferred because:
    • 1× standard deviation captures approximately 68% of data within normal range, flagging the top ~16% as potential outliers
    • This threshold is sensitive enough to catch projects that deviate significantly from the norm
    • Projects above this threshold are likely to require closer scrutiny for cost efficiency
  • Per Category/Subcategory: Each category (Bridges, Road Safety Facilities subcategories, Major/Minor Roads work types) has its own threshold calculated from projects in that specific category
  • Flagging Rule: A project is flagged if its cost/km exceeds the threshold for its category
  • Composite Projects ("Average of Average"): For projects with multiple components (e.g., "Widening with Asphalt Overlay"), statistics use the average cost per component (total cost/km divided by number of components). However, flagging uses the original total cost/km against the threshold to ensure accurate outlier detection.

This methodology ensures that flagging is contextually appropriate - a high-cost project in one category may be normal, while the same cost in another category would be an outlier.
