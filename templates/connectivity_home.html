How to read the map. Road and bridge rows are FY2027 House Bill projects matched by municipality names in their titles; ambiguous or unnamed rows are not assigned. This is a text match, not a geocoded alignment, and a barangay sharing a municipality's name can still create a false match. FMR proposals are placed where their printed route endpoints fall inside a municipality boundary. These are proposed budget records, not verified construction or spending.
