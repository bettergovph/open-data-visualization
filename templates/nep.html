🚨 NEP 2026 Analysis: Flood Control Pattern Detection

Using the known Bulacan flood control scheme as a baseline to identify similar suspicious patterns in NEP 2026

⚠️ Red Flag Detected

Road infrastructure spending in NEP 2026 shows a +61.9% increase vs historical average (2020-2025), exceeding the flood control pattern threshold of ≥50% YoY growth.

🔴 Red Flag: Road Infrastructure (2020-2026)

+61.9% increase vs historical average exceeds flood control threshold (≥50%)

📊 Flood Control Baseline: Bulacan Pattern (2020-2024)

Reference pattern: +118% growth (2022→2023) with geographic concentration

🔍 Key Findings

✅ Overall Budget Growth

+7.4% (Normal)

✅ Geographic Distribution

27.2% in top 3 regions (Better than flood's 60%)

🚨 Road Infrastructure

+61.9% increase (Exceeds 50% threshold)

✅ Flood Control Spending

+33.6% (Below threshold)

📋 Methodology

This analysis uses the known Bulacan flood control scheme (2020-2024) as a baseline pattern. The Bulacan pattern showed +118% growth in 2022→2023 with 60% geographic concentration. We compared NEP 2026 against Budget history (2020-2025) to identify similar patterns. Threshold: ≥50% YoY growth is flagged as matching the flood control scheme.
